SuperWhisper had no Windows version, so I built my own. Whisper4Windows runs OpenAI's
Whisper models entirely on your machine: no account, no API key and no audio sent anywhere.

## Two processes

The app is split in two. A Rust and Tauri front end owns the hotkeys, the small recording window and
its live waveform. A Python FastAPI service owns the model, so the interface never waits on it
while a large model loads or runs.

## Fast, then reliable

Keeping the Whisper model cached between dictations is what makes it quick: 30 seconds of speech comes
back as text in under a second on a GPU.

## Making it work on any PC

Windows machines vary a lot, and model loading was the most common failure. The service checks for
CUDA through CTranslate2 and degrades gracefully: from GPU to CPU inference when it has to, and from
float16 to int8 when the hardware can't handle the larger format.

## Typing into any app

Simulating keystrokes one character at a time is slow and breaks in some apps. Instead, the text goes
through the Win32 clipboard APIs and a single Ctrl+V, and the app can restore whatever was on your
clipboard before.
