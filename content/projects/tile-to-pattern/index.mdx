Patterned AI generates images that tile. Tile To Pattern is the step after that: drop in one tile and
Canva gets a full, editable pattern that repeats without visible seams.

## How it works

You upload a PNG, JPG or SVG tile and pick a layout: a straight grid, a brick offset or a half drop.
Zoom, horizontal position and vertical position are live sliders, and the preview shows exactly what
will land in the design. The output can fill the page or use a custom size, so the same tile works
for a phone wallpaper or a print.

## Shipping it fast

The previous Patterned AI Canva app took months. This one took three weeks, mostly because I leaned on
agentic automation through MCP servers for the repetitive parts of the Canva Apps SDK work. It passed
1,500 installs and 600 active users in its first month, and has since grown past 15,000 monthly
active users.

## The data side

The same internship covered the training data behind the tiles. I built a sitemap-driven scraping and
filtering pipeline in JavaScript and Python that curated more than 150,000 images, with edge-adjacency
checks that throw out tiles that don't actually repeat seamlessly. Tuning it to run across 10 parallel
workers took it to more than 30,000 images an hour, about ten times the old throughput, with 95% fewer
failed jobs.
