One tile is enough for a pattern. Arranging hundreds of copies by hand shouldn't be part of the job. I built Tile To Pattern so people could do that work directly in Canva.

## Getting the repeat right

Upload a tile, choose a straight grid or staggered layout, then adjust its size and position. The preview updates as you work. A seamless source tile stays seamless in the finished pattern, whether it fills the page or uses custom dimensions.

## Three weeks to launch

I built the app with React, TypeScript and the Canva Apps SDK. Coding agents helped with repetitive SDK work, bringing development down to three weeks. It reached 1,500 installs in its first month and later passed 15,000 monthly active users.

The small demo on this page uses geometric tiles to show how the layouts repeat. The Canva app works with uploaded PNG, JPG and SVG images.
