Canva's AI and Integrations Hackathon asked for apps built on its Apps SDK. We were a team of two with two weeks in August 2024. My teammate wrote the Python service; I built the Canva app and the contract between the two. The idea: charts without leaving Canva.

## A job, not a request

Generating charts from a spreadsheet takes longer than a browser request should wait, so the FastAPI service on Modal works as a job queue, and the app talks to it in three calls:

1. `POST /get-upload-url` returns a presigned S3 link, and the app uploads the file straight to storage, so the spreadsheet never passes through the API.
2. `POST /generate-plots-request` starts a job for that file and returns a call ID right away.
3. `GET /get-plots/{id}` answers 202 while the job runs and 200 with the charts once it's done. The app polls it every 2 seconds and shows progress meanwhile.

## Charts as data

The service doesn't send back images. It returns Plotly figures as JSON, so the app renders them itself and every chart stays editable: title, axis labels, fonts, colours and backgrounds can all change in the app's edit panel. Only the version you keep becomes a PNG in your design.

The app takes CSV, XLS and XLSX files up to 10 MB.
