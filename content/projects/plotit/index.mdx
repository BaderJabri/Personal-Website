Most people who need a chart in Canva start with a spreadsheet. Plotit skips the manual part: upload a
CSV and it picks out the columns worth plotting and returns charts you can keep editing in your design.

## How it works

The Canva side is a React app built on the Canva Apps SDK. It sends the file to a FastAPI backend that
cleans and profiles the data, chooses chart types, and renders them with Plotly. Most of the work went
into the request and response flow between the two, so that large CSV files still come back quickly.

A chart that took around 20 minutes to build by hand comes back in about 15 seconds.
